Our Story

How It All Began

In 2024, our story began in the simplest of ways.

My daughter asked me if I could make a Mattah for her. I made one for her simply out of love, as her mother. Looking back, I believe God was already preparing us for something He had placed in our hearts.

God has called us to use our gifts to serve and bless others — creating dance instruments and special Mattahs that help people express their love for God through worship and dance.

01 — HIS HANDS

My husband prepares the wood

He makes sure that every piece is strong, beautifully shaped, and easy to carry.

02 — MY CREATIVITY

I bring the design to life

Creativity, color, prayer, and inspiration come together to bring each design to life.

03 — TOGETHER

A partnership God has given us

Together, we make every Mattah with love, care, joy, passion, and purpose.

It started with one Mattah for our daughter.
But God had a much bigger plan. ❤️

Meet the Maker

Crafted with purpose. Created in worship.

Marivia Antonia was born in Curaçao and has made the Netherlands her home. Her family, faith, creativity, and ministry remain the foundation of Prophetic Crafting.

01 — HER STORY

A creative soul & devoted Christian

Creating meaningful worship tools with care, prayer, and intention.

02 — HER HEART

More than a craft

Each piece is an act of worship and a way to bring life and joy to ministry.

03 — HER CALLING

Equipping worshippers

Helping dancers and worship leaders minister through movement with meaningful tools.

Our Vision

Creativity as a language of faith.

Prophetic Crafting is about giving visible form to what God places in your heart. Through symbolism, creativity, prayer, and room for the Holy Spirit, we create work that is not only beautiful, but also speaks.
